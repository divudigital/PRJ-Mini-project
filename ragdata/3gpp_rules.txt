3GPP NETWORK GUIDELINES

RSRP < -110 dBm:
Recommended action:
Immediate handover or power boost.

Latency > 50 ms:
Recommended action:
Traffic steering and slice reallocation.

Jitter > 20 ms:
Recommended action:
Buffer optimization and route balancing.

Packet Loss > 5%:
Recommended action:
Priority QoS scheduling.

SINR < 5 dB:
Recommended action:
Coverage optimization.
